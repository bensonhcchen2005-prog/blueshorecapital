Strategy Breakdown
MA Crossover LONG
Trend-following. Enters when SMA(20) crosses above SMA(50) with RSI > 50 and adequate volume. Captures sustained momentum shifts after moving average alignment.
Entry: SMA20 > SMA50 cross RSI > 50 RVOL > 0.8
Exit: Stop: SMA50 - 0.5x ATR Target: +2.5x ATR
Momentum LONG
Trend continuation. Enters on strong rate-of-change (>3%) with positive and accelerating MACD, price above SMA50, RSI 40-75. Rides established uptrends with wide stops.
Entry: ROC > 3% MACD accel Price > SMA50 RSI 40-75
Exit: Stop: -2x ATR Target: +3x ATR
Short Momentum SHORT
Downtrend capture. Shorts when price is below SMA20, 20-day momentum < -5, RSI weakening but not yet oversold (25-55). US markets only. Counter-balances long book.
Entry: Price < SMA20 Mom20 < -5 RSI 25-55
Exit: Stop: SMA20 + ATR Target: -2.5x ATR
Mean Reversion LONG
Counter-trend. Buys oversold bounces at Bollinger Band lower bound with RSI < 32 confirmation. Targets mean (BB midline). Currently underperforming — catching falling knives in trending markets.
Entry: RSI < 32 Price near BB lower RVOL > 0.8
Exit: Stop: -2.5x ATR Target: BB midline (SMA20)
Breakout LONG
Momentum burst. Enters when price breaks 20-bar high with elevated volume (1.3x+) and trend confirmation (price > SMA50). Anti-chase filter prevents late entries.
Entry: 20-bar high break RVOL > 1.3 Price > SMA50
Exit: Stop: Breakout - 0.5x ATR Target: +2.5x ATR
RSI Divergence LONG
Reversal detection. Spots bullish divergence: price makes lower low while RSI makes higher low. Catches bottoms before MAs react. High reward when correct; many false signals.
Entry: RSI bullish divergence RSI < 50 Price < SMA20
Exit: Stop: Recent low - ATR Target: SMA20
Gap Reversal LONG
Intraday mean-reversion. Buys gap-down opens (>2%) that recover >50% within the same bar. Targets full gap fill. Rarely triggers on daily bars — designed for intraday.
Entry: Gap down > 2% Recovery > 50% Green candle
Exit: Stop: Session low - 0.5x ATR Target: Prior close
